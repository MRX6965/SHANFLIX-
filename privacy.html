Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we store on your device

SHANFLIX saves your My List, Continue Watching progress and recent searches in your browser's local storage so they are there next time you visit. This data stays on your device unless you choose to sign in for sync (below). You can clear it any time by clearing site data.

Optional account and sync

If cloud sync is enabled and you sign in with your email, we store your email address and your My List, progress and recent searches so they can follow you across devices. We do not sell this data. Contact us to have it deleted.

Third-party services

  • TMDB supplies titles, posters and descriptions. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • Video players are embedded from third-party providers. They load in their own frames and may set their own cookies and collect data under their own policies. SHANFLIX does not host video files.
  • Hosting and fonts are provided by Vercel and Google Fonts, which may log your IP address.
  • Advertising: embedded players or links may show ads from third parties.

Children

SHANFLIX is not directed to children under 13.
